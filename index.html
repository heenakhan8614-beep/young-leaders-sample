Everything Parents Ask Before Booking.

What age group are the workshops for?

Most workshops are for ages 5–12; some are for ages 8–12.

Where do workshops take place?

At various Delhi NCR venues. The exact location is shown for the event you select.

How long is a workshop?

It varies. Check the specific workshop card.

What should my child bring?

A water bottle, and a snack where appropriate. Activity-specific details are shared ahead.

Is pick-up and drop-off available?

Not offered as a regular service.

What happens if plans or weather change?

Our team contacts parents with updated arrangements.

How do I confirm the booking?

Select a session and complete secure payment in the applicable booking journey.

Not sure which workshop fits?

Tell us your child’s age and interests and we’ll suggest suitable options.
